# 24 — WP4 offline pricing dry-run, 2026-11-10

The deploy gate from `20-mvp1-scope.md` §6 WP4: one real day of production toll readings, replayed
into toll events and priced by the code on this branch. **Status: run; awaiting the second
person's review the plan requires before any production deploy.** This file holds aggregates only. The
per-traversal output (`data prod/toll_pricing_dryrun_2026-11-10/`) carries device coordinates and
stays out of git.

## Method

- **Input.** `data prod/export_sep3.tsv`: the toll_event_trend.sql section 13 export for 2026-09-03,
  237,252 positions from 2,337 devices. Every position in it is valid and was looked up (0 failed);
  23,136 are stale. Fix times run from 2026-08-25 to 2026-09-03, because stale positions arrive late.
- **Detection.** `PricingDryRun` (`src/test/.../tollpricing`, runs only when `TOLL_DRYRUN_EXPORT` is
  set) drives the production `TollRouteState` and `TollRouteProcessor` the way `TollEventHandler` does:
  - each device starts with fresh state;
  - positions are fed in arrival (id) order;
  - stale and invalid positions are dropped;
  - minimalDuration is 4 (production since 2026-09-07);
  - events are schema 2: the enter points at the run's first confirmation and the exit at its last.
- **Pricing.** The harness pairs each exit with its enter exactly as `TaskTollPricing` does: events
  in eventTime order, a 12 h lookback, and a trail read from the previous exit when that exit is
  within 15 min. It then prices through the production `TraversalBuilder` and the packaged registry
  (calc version 1).
- **Fidelity check.** It simulates **385 enters**, the same figure `replay_window.py` gives for this
  day at duration 4. That script was itself matched against production (320 simulated vs 312 actual
  enters at the duration in force that day).

## Accuracy limitation: sparse trails miss toll points

A mainline toll point is charged only when a trail segment passes within 50 m of it
(`PassageDetector.THRESHOLD_M`). Many devices report every 1.5 to 12 minutes, and a straight chord
between two such fixes cuts the road's curves.

- **55 of 235 flat traversals (23%)** pass a mainline toll point of a tested facility at 50-250 m:
  - 82 toll points in all: 53 at 50-100 m, 14 at 100-150 m, 15 at 150-250 m;
  - 37 of those traversals are priced (so priced low) and 18 are `NO_PASSAGES`.
- The median segment at those near misses is 120 s and 3.5 km.
- Most are on SR 417, SR 528 and SR 408 (CFX), plus E-470 and Illinois.

**Not every near miss is a real passage.** Some are a plaza on a neighbouring road (for example
I 4 East trips passing CFX plazas). So simply widening the threshold would invent charges as well as
recover them. The fix that removes the guess is matching the trail to the road. That is D6
(self-hosted Valhalla, decided 2026-08-24). Until then, flat-facility estimates are a **lower bound**
whenever fixes are sparse, and `trailPoints` in each row's inputs shows how sparse.

Other things this export cannot show, all of which push the dry-run low compared with production:

- it holds only looked-up positions (500 m gate that day), so trails are thinner than `tc_positions`;
- fresh state at midnight;
- duration 4 against a day that ran at 6.
